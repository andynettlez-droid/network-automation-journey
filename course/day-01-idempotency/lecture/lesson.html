Day 1 — Idempotency

Give a router’s port an address with automation — and make re-running change nothing.

★ Concepts to remember

IdempotencyDeclare the state you want. Re-running changes nothing once the device matches.
Declarative > imperativeState what should be true, not step-by-step commands. That’s what makes it idempotent.
Ground truth“Success” is a claim; the device is the proof. Always verify on the device.
DEMONSTRATION — 1 / 5

✓ Quick recap — check yourself

1. Run 1 said changed=1, run 2 said changed=0. Is your automation idempotent?

2. The tool says “success” but the device disagrees. Which do you trust?

3. Which style is safe to re-run — declarative or imperative? Why?
