# Lesson format

Every lesson follows the same four beats. **Audio carries the concept and the "why"; the screen carries the commands and the truth.**

## 1. What & Why — narrated audio + text
A short spoken intro: what we're about to do and why it matters. Generated by `tools/narrate.py` (ElevenLabs) and played before the hands-on work. **Concept and reasoning only — never commands, IPs, or YAML** (a misheard character is a silent bug). The same text is shown on screen.

## 2. Demonstrate — I drive
I run the steps in the terminal and show the real output, narrating what's happening. You watch the moving parts before touching them (the "I-do").

## 3. You do — your hands
You repeat it yourself: write the playbook, run it, induce the fault. The struggle is the learning; I unstick the exact point only after you've attempted it.

## 4. Verify — ground truth
We check the result on the device itself (`sr_cli`, telemetry) — never the tool's own success line. The gap between what the automation *claims* and what the device *shows* is the whole skill.

## Producing the audio
- The what/why script lives at `lessons-audio/<lesson>.txt`.
- Generate: `python3 tools/narrate.py lessons-audio/<lesson>.txt`
- Output `.mp3` lands in `lessons-audio/` (gitignored — it's regenerated from the script and costs API credits, so generate once).
- Key + voice come from a gitignored `.env` at the repo root: `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY=...` and optionally `ELEVEN_VOICE_ID=...`.
